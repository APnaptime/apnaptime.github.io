Background

I wanted a 3D printer. In addition to a hobby, a home printer is a useful machine for "maker" style project and home DIY endeavors. My first printer was an $200 Ender 3 v2 back in 2022. I made several 3D printed PCBless mechanical keyboards with that printer and did some slight modifications to it as well. In general that first ender was not great - slow, hard to use and a pain to level and calibrate. I've since moved and gifted that old printer to my younger brother. He then noteably got a different printer and I did much the same. My current printing hobby solution / home appliance for making phyiscal objects is a Prusa Mini - a smaller footprint and build volume but much easier to use and service. I printed a few home improvement projects with it such as a set of shelf brackets for my now very small kitchen and some wall mounted coat hangers. It is a great printer but I do find myself limited in the size of the objects I can make. It also has the unfortunate characteristic of being unenclosed resulting in being restricted to more basic filaments - sufficient for aesthetic and some light load bearing tasks but not enough for stronger and more exotic plastics.

Knowing that I wanted a printer, the question is now which one? This choice is usually influenced by a variety of factors. In a potentially particular order: availability, budget constraints, capability, durability, repairability, and fun factor. At the time of writing - August, 2026 - most folks looking into a non-first filament style printer fit into 4 broad categories. First you've got the two big ecosystems / brands, Prusa (Prusa Research) and Bambu (Bambu Lab).
